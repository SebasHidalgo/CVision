Marketing Manager - Harvest Table Foods (Chicago, IL, hybrid)

Harvest Table Foods makes pantry staples sold in 3,000 grocery stores and online. We are hiring a Marketing Manager to lead campaigns for our two largest product lines.

Responsibilities
- Own the marketing plan and budget for two product lines, from annual planning to weekly execution.
- Lead integrated campaigns across paid social, email, retail media and in-store promotions.
- Turn consumer research and sales data into positioning and messaging.
- Manage external agencies and freelancers, and brief the in-house creative team.
- Report campaign performance and return on spend to leadership every month.
- Partner with sales and retail teams on trade promotions and product launches.

Requirements
- 5+ years of marketing experience in consumer packaged goods, retail or a consumer brand.
- Proven record of planning and running integrated campaigns with a budget of $1M or more.
- Hands-on experience with paid social, email marketing platforms and web analytics (GA4).
- Experience managing agencies or vendors.
- Clear written communication and comfort presenting to senior leaders.

Nice to have
- Experience with retail media networks or trade marketing.
- Familiarity with SQL or BI tools such as Looker Studio or Tableau.
- Product launch experience in the food and beverage category.

Salary range $95,000 - $115,000 plus bonus. Hybrid, three days a week in our Chicago office.
