Product Engineer (Full-stack)
Aurelia Labs | Remote (Europe)

About the role
We are a twelve-person product team building scheduling software for clinics. Product
engineers here own features from the interface down to the database, and we expect you
to be opinionated about both.

Our stack
- React and TypeScript on the front end.
- Node.js services behind a GraphQL gateway.
- PostgreSQL, with schema changes reviewed by the whole team.
- AWS, provisioned with Terraform.
- Jest for unit tests, Cypress for end-to-end.

What we are looking for
- 4+ years building web applications professionally.
- Strong React and TypeScript.
- Comfort writing and reviewing SQL against a relational database.
- Experience with GraphQL schema design, or a clear appetite to learn it fast.
- Familiarity with infrastructure as code; you will be reading Terraform in your first
  month even if you do not write much of it.
- A testing habit, whatever the framework.

Nice to have
- Experience in healthcare or another regulated domain.
- Experience with Cypress specifically.
- An eye for accessibility, especially for older users.

How we hire
A take-home exercise you will be paid for, then two conversations: one technical, one
about how you work. No whiteboard algorithms.
