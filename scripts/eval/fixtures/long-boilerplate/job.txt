Customer Support Team Lead
Orbit Telecom · Dublin, Ireland · Permanent, full-time

Our story
Orbit Telecom was founded in 2009 by three engineers who believed that a phone bill should fit on one page. Seventeen years later we serve 1.4 million customers across Ireland with mobile, broadband and TV, and that first bill still fits on one page. We are owned by our employees and by a long-term infrastructure fund, which lets us plan in years rather than quarters.

Our values
Straight talk. Customers hear the truth from us, even when it costs a sale.
Fix it twice. Solve the customer's problem, then the cause of it.
No heroes. Sustainable workloads, documented processes, and leaders who take the late shift too.

The team
Our customer support organisation is 140 people across phone, chat and email, working from Dublin and from home. Team leads run groups of eight to twelve agents and are the people our agents learn the job from.

The role
We are hiring a Customer Support Team Lead for our broadband queue. The person we hire has led a support team of at least five people for two or more years, writes clearly in English and Spanish because a growing share of our customers prefer Spanish, and knows Zendesk or a comparable helpdesk tool well enough to build views and macros without help. Experience with workforce scheduling and QA scorecards is important to us, since you will own both for your group. A background in telecommunications would help but is not essential; most of our best leads came from retail, hospitality or utilities.

Day to day you will coach agents through call reviews, run the weekly quality calibration, own the schedule for your group, and be the escalation point for the hardest customer cases. You report to the Head of Support and meet the other team leads daily.

What we offer
Salary from €48,000 to €56,000 depending on experience, a 10% annual bonus tied to customer satisfaction, 25 days of leave rising to 30, a health plan for you and your family, a pension matched up to 6%, a free Orbit mobile and broadband plan, and a learning allowance of €1,500 a year. We work three days in the office and two from home, and we close the office between Christmas and New Year.

How we hire
A 30-minute call with a recruiter, a conversation with the Head of Support, and a half-day with the team where you will review two recorded calls with us. We reply to every applicant within ten working days.
