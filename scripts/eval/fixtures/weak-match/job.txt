Senior Backend Engineer - Vantage Payments (Remote, US)

Vantage Payments processes card payments for 40,000 merchants. Our Core Platform team owns the services that authorize, settle and reconcile every transaction. We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer.

What you will do
- Design, build and operate high-throughput services in Go.
- Own services end to end on Kubernetes, including on-call for the systems you build.
- Design APIs and data models for payment authorization and settlement.
- Improve reliability: observability, load testing and incident follow-ups.
- Mentor engineers and lead technical design reviews.

Requirements
- 6+ years of professional backend software development.
- Strong experience with Go, Java or another statically typed language.
- Experience with distributed systems, message queues (Kafka) and relational databases (PostgreSQL).
- Production experience with Kubernetes and a major cloud provider (AWS or GCP).
- Experience with observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana or Datadog.

Nice to have
- Payments or fintech experience, including PCI DSS.
- Experience with event sourcing or CQRS.

Compensation $180,000 - $220,000 plus equity.
