Backend Developer - Internal Platform Team
Northwind Freight | Remote (Latin America)

About the role
Our internal platform team builds the services the rest of the company runs on:
shipment tracking, rating, and the APIs our carrier partners integrate with. You would
own one of those services end to end, alongside four other backend developers.

Required qualifications
- 3+ years of professional backend development experience.
- Experience writing production code in languages including, but not limited to, C,
  C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python.
- Hands-on experience with relational databases and SQL.
- Experience with containers and CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience designing and maintaining REST APIs.
- Working proficiency in written and spoken English.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience running Kubernetes in production.
- Experience with event-driven systems such as Kafka or RabbitMQ.
- Exposure to Go or Rust.
- Experience with observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry).
- An AWS or GCP certification.
- Experience in logistics, freight or supply chain.

What the first six months look like
You pick up one existing service, learn its failure modes on call, and take the lead on
its next version. After that you will have a say in which parts of the platform we
rebuild and in what order.

We review applications on a rolling basis and reply to every candidate.
