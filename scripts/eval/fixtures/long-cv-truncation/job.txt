Staff Backend Engineer - Cobalt Freight Network (Remote, US or Canada)

Cobalt Freight Network connects shippers with 90,000 carriers across North America. Our platform prices, books and tracks more than two million shipments a month. We are hiring a Staff Backend Engineer to shape the architecture of our shipment lifecycle services.

What you will do
- Set technical direction for the services that price, book and track shipments.
- Design distributed systems that stay correct under partial failure: idempotent APIs, event-driven workflows, exactly-once processing where it matters.
- Lead the evolution of our Kafka-based event platform and our PostgreSQL data layer.
- Drive reliability: SLOs, observability, capacity planning and incident reviews.
- Mentor senior engineers and raise the bar on design documents and code review.
- Work with product and data teams to turn ambiguous problems into roadmaps.

What we are looking for
- 10+ years of backend engineering, with at least 3 at a senior or staff level.
- Deep experience with Go, Java or Kotlin in production.
- Proven experience designing event-driven systems with Kafka or a similar log.
- Strong PostgreSQL skills, including schema design, query tuning and migrations at scale.
- Production experience with Kubernetes on AWS or GCP, and infrastructure as code (Terraform).
- A track record of leading cross-team technical initiatives.
- A degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.

Nice to have
- Logistics, marketplace or payments domain experience.
- Experience with stream processing (Flink, Kafka Streams).
- Public speaking or open-source contributions.

Compensation $210,000 - $250,000 plus equity. Remote in the US or Canada.
