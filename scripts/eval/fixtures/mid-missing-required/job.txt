Senior Backend Engineer, Data Platform
Fenwick Analytics | Hybrid, Manchester

About the role
The data platform team owns the pipelines that every analytics product in the company
reads from. You would work on the ingestion side: the services that pull customer data
in, validate it, and land it in the warehouse.

Required qualifications
- 5+ years of professional backend engineering experience.
- 3+ years writing production Python. This is the language the whole platform is
  written in, and the team does not have the capacity to support a different runtime.
- Experience with a cloud provider (AWS, GCP or Azure) in production.
- Experience with relational databases and schema design.
- Experience being on call for a production service.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with Airflow or a comparable orchestrator.
- Experience with dbt and warehouse modelling.
- Experience with Kafka or another streaming platform.
- Experience in a regulated industry.

How we work
Small team, long-lived services, and a strong preference for boring technology. Code
review is mandatory and design documents precede anything that will outlive a quarter.

We are unable to consider candidates without commercial Python experience for this
particular role, as the team is small and everything in production is Python.
