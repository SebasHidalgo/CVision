Frontend Engineer - Meridian Health (Remote, US)

About the role
Meridian Health builds scheduling and intake software used by 2,000 clinics. We are looking for a Frontend Engineer to join the team that owns our patient-facing web app. You will ship features used by millions of patients a year and help raise the bar on performance and accessibility.

What you will do
- Build and maintain features in our React and TypeScript web application.
- Own the performance of key flows (booking, intake forms) and track Core Web Vitals.
- Make every screen accessible; we target WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Collaborate with product designers on our component library.
- Write unit and end-to-end tests and keep the suite fast and reliable.
- Review code and mentor less experienced engineers.

What we are looking for
- 3+ years of professional experience with React and TypeScript.
- Experience with Next.js or another server-side rendering framework.
- Strong CSS skills; experience with Tailwind CSS or a design system.
- Experience with automated testing (Jest or Vitest, and Playwright or Cypress).
- Working knowledge of web accessibility standards.

Nice to have
- GraphQL experience.
- Experience in healthcare or another regulated industry.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines such as GitHub Actions.

What we offer
Fully remote team, four weeks of paid time off, a learning budget and a yearly team offsite.
