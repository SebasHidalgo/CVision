Data Analyst - Northpoint Retail Group (Remote, EMEA)

We are a fast-growing online retailer operating in six European countries. Our Data team supports merchandising, operations and marketing, and we are hiring a Data Analyst to own reporting for our commercial teams.

What you will do
- Build and maintain dashboards (Power BI or Looker) for sales, margin and returns.
- Write SQL to answer business questions and automate recurring reports.
- Analyze A/B tests and explain the results to non-technical stakeholders.
- Support demand forecasting with the data science team.
- Improve data quality and keep metric definitions consistent across teams.

What we are looking for
- 3+ years of experience as a data or business analyst, ideally in e-commerce or retail.
- Strong SQL; experience with a cloud warehouse such as BigQuery or Snowflake.
- Python for analysis (pandas).
- Experience with a BI tool, preferably Power BI.
- Solid understanding of statistics and experiment design.
- Fluent English; our working language is English. Another European language is a plus.

We offer remote work within EMEA, a yearly learning budget and flexible hours.
